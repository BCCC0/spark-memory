# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, constructive, and collaborative. Critique ideas and code,
not people. Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and unwanted sexualized
attention are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner. Reports will be
reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially.
